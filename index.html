01 · ABOUT

Safety is a discipline I build, not a box I check.

My career started on the ground floor of India's construction industry, where I learned early that a safe site is never accidental — it is engineered, inspected and reinforced every single day. Over the past one-plus years with Ahluwalia Contractors on the Okhla Industrial Area project, I've moved from carrying out toolbox talks to owning permit-to-work systems, incident investigations and compliance audits across a live, high-tempo site.

My mission is simple: reduce risk before it becomes an incident, and build a culture where every worker — from a fresh labourer to a project director — genuinely believes safety is their responsibility too. My vision is to carry that same discipline into the Gulf's international construction industry, where standards are exacting and the margin for error is smaller still.

I'm currently completing my NEBOSH IGC, targeting a Distinction.
